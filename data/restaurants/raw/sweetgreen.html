Allergen information

Heads up — Our salmon’s marinade temporarily contains wheat (gluten).

Sweetgreen tracks nine major allergens across menu items: dairy, eggs, fish, shellfish, wheat, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, and sesame.

Sweetgreen kitchens are not allergen-free environments and cross-contamination may occur. Please inform your in-store team member of any food allergies.
